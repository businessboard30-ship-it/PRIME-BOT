Music · FIELD NOTES 058

Betty is built for music-first Discord servers. How far does its sound go?

A closer look at Betty's playback, Spotify sync, supported music services, long queues and paid custom-bot options.

DISCORD BOT REVIEWER EDITORIAL9 NOVEMBER 20265 MIN READ
BETTY · MUSIC SPOTLIGHT

Some Discord bots try to do everything. Betty takes a different route: it puts music at the centre, then builds its experience around playback quality, playlist management and control over how music runs in a server.

A music bot with a clear identity

Betty's official site describes it as a Discord music bot focused on high-quality audio, Spotify support and 24/7 playback. It also promotes a yearly listening recap called Your Wrapped. The narrow focus can be an advantage: a music bot should make it easy to find tracks, manage a queue and keep playback dependable without burying the basics under unrelated modules.

Playback and platform support

The official site highlights Spotify playlist syncing and the ability to like songs from Discord. Betty's documentation and bot listings describe support for services including Tidal, Apple Music, Amazon Music, Deezer, SoundCloud and Bandcamp. YouTube links are accepted when the track is available through another supported platform. Source availability can change, so test the platforms your members actually use.

Queues, playlists and premium

The public pricing section advertises a monthly plan at €2.50 and an annual plan equivalent to €2.08 per month. Listed benefits include custom playlists up to 2,500 songs, queues up to 25,000 songs, lyrics, duplicate-song prevention, member cleanup, design customization and a premium audio node. A custom-bot tier offers a personalized username and profile picture, premium features for up to two servers and three bot instances. Confirm current prices and limits at checkout.

What to check before inviting

  • Source compatibility: test the platforms and link types your community uses.
  • Queue behaviour: try playlist imports, long queues, skips and reconnects.
  • 24/7 playback: confirm availability and any premium restrictions.
  • Permissions: inspect the invite and grant only what music playback requires.
  • Premium terms: compare monthly, annual and custom-bot plans.

Our take

Betty's strength is focus. Spotify playlist sync, large queues, custom playlists and optional custom bot identities make it more than a basic play command. The public premium tiers also give users a reasonably clear idea of what they are paying for.

The caveat is that music-source support can change, and a feature list does not prove every link will work at every moment. We have not independently benchmarked audio quality, latency or uptime. Betty is worth testing for music-led communities; it is not trying to replace a full moderation or server-management suite.

Best suited to: servers that want a dedicated music bot with playlist features, long queues and optional premium customization.
