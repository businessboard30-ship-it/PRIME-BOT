Quill & Ink is building a modular toolkit for Discord communities.

A plugin-based bot combining moderation, tickets, voice tools, economy, games, server stores and cross-community collaboration.

DISCORD BOT REVIEWER EDITORIAL9 NOVEMBER 20265 MIN READ
QUILL & INK · COMMUNITY SPOTLIGHT

Quill & Ink takes the modular-bot approach: rather than presenting itself as one narrow utility, it groups a wide set of community features into plugins that administrators can configure from a setup portal.

One bot, several plugin categories

Its public descriptions list more than 18 plugins spanning moderation, logging, voice, tickets, economy and games. The economy is branded around Nickel and includes wallets, banking, transactions and server-configurable systems. Interactive games and a robbery system add an entertainment layer, while the store plugin lets communities offer their own products or rewards.

Community features beyond one server

Quill & Ink also promotes global transactions and collaboration announcements. These features aim to connect communities without requiring every member to join multiple servers. That is an interesting angle for networks and partner communities, although administrators should understand what information crosses server boundaries and how each integration is controlled.

Free core, optional premium

The public listing describes the bot as free to use, with some advanced plugins and customization behind Premium. It advertises Premium at ₹350 for 30 days. Check the current plan details for which plugins and limits are included before relying on a paid feature.

What to test before inviting

  • Plugin control: confirm each module can be enabled, configured and disabled independently.
  • Economy safety: inspect transaction logs, store controls, transfer rules and anti-abuse settings.
  • Cross-server features: understand what global transactions and announcements expose or share.
  • Permissions: review the Discord authorization screen and avoid granting broad access without a reason.
  • Support and maintenance: check recent updates and ask how plugin issues are handled.

Our take

Quill & Ink's strongest idea is its modular scope. It tries to bring practical administration and member-facing entertainment into one configurable app, with a distinct economy and collaboration layer. That could reduce the need to install several separate bots, provided the modules remain reliable and understandable.

We have not independently tested uptime, permission boundaries, economy balancing or cross-server transaction safety. Test the modules you need in a private server, and verify current pricing and availability in the official setup flow.

Best suited to: communities that want a broad, configurable bot with economy and games alongside moderation and support tools.
