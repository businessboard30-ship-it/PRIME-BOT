How it works

Three steps

Invite

Click Add to Discord, pick your server and approve the permissions. Why each permission is requested.

One-tap setup DM

The server owner receives one DM with a Turn on button for each feature: welcome, verification, tickets, leveling, auto-moderation and more. Only the owner or someone with Manage Server can use them.

Done

Fine-tune everything later from /serversetup, and browse every feature with /help. Missed the DM? Run /start.

FAQ

Quick answers

Is it really free?
Yes. Adding and using the bot is free. Paid features are optional: Go Premium (per server) and a handful of one-time unlocks. All prices are in USD.
What does Go Premium cost?
$2.99/month, $16/year (about 55% off the monthly price: $16 instead of $35.88 for 12 months) or $99.99 lifetime, one-time. It applies to the whole server, not to one member.
Is there a discount?
Yes: the first time a person pays for Premium, the Yearly plan is 50% off ($8 instead of $16). It is one offer per person and is applied automatically when you choose it in /premium.
How do I buy Premium?
Run /premium in your server, or tap the Go Premium button in the setup DM or in /help. The checkout is handled by the payment provider, not by this website.
How does billing work?
Global checkout is through Gumroad: the monthly plan renews automatically and can be cancelled any time. Local checkout is through Paystack: each payment covers 30 days and never renews on its own. Yearly and lifetime are one-time payments.
Can I get a refund?
Yes, in the situations described in the Refund Policy: for example a payment that was never activated, or a feature that does not work as described because of a fault on our side.
What data does the bot store?
Discord IDs needed for the features you use, message content only where a feature requires it (such as auto-moderation filters), and payment references (not card details). We do not sell your data. Read the Privacy Policy.
Who runs this?
Maxwell Dumenya, an independent developer. See About or email maxwelldumenya5@outlook.com.
