Data Deletion

PRIME BOT × WELCOME BOT · Last updated 27 October 2026

You decide what stays. This page lists every way to delete the data PRIME BOT × WELCOME BOT holds about you or your server, what is removed, what we have to keep and why, and how long short-lived records last. For what we collect in the first place, read the Privacy Policy.

1 · Run /mydata Fastest

Run /mydata in any server with the bot, or in a DM with it. It shows what is stored against your Discord account and lets you delete it yourself. To confirm, type DELETE MY DATA. Nothing is removed until you do.

2 · Web dashboard

Sign in with Discord, open Privacy & data and delete your dashboard data: level-up card and uploads, saved AI provider keys, export receipts, scheduled jobs, renewal reminders, friends list and member messages. Disconnecting GitHub deletes its key and revokes it at GitHub.

3 · Remove the bot from a server

If you manage a server, kick the bot or remove it from your integrations. Everything stored for that server is deleted automatically after a 14-day grace period, so an accidental removal can be undone: re-add the bot within 14 days and everything comes back.

4 · Leave a server

When you leave, or are removed from, a server the bot is in, your records for that server only are deleted automatically. Your other servers and your account-level data are not touched; use /mydata for those.

5 · Ask us to do it

If you cannot use the commands above, or want to opt out of having your activity tracked, email maxwelldumenya5@outlook.com with:

  • your Discord user ID (and the server ID if the request is about one server),
  • whether you want everything deleted or only certain data,
  • a way to confirm the request comes from you, for example a message from the Discord account in question.

We will delete the data, or tell you why we cannot (for example payment records we must keep).

Good to know

  • Deletion cannot be undone, apart from the 14-day server grace period.
  • AI chats on the website are not stored; only a weekly count is kept.
  • Bots run from this service, including white-label bots such as Welcome Bot, follow the same rules.
  • Copies held by third parties (Discord, Gumroad, Paystack, your AI provider) are governed by their own policies; ask them directly.
