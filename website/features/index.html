Features

Everything the bot does

Each feature below is shipped in the bot today. “Free” means included with the free bot; “Premium” means part of Go Premium; “Paid unlock” means a separate one-time purchase.

Welcome & onboarding FreePremium

Greet new members with generated welcome cards and messages, say goodbye, and hand out roles automatically.

  • Welcome cards with themes, avatar shapes and stickers
  • Goodbye message with channel, text and test post
  • Member and bot auto-roles
  • Premium: extra card themes and your own custom background (also sold as one-time packs)
  • Customize Card wizard with live preview: 11 fonts, optional brackets around the member name, layouts, colors and style presets

/welcome /serversetup

Join verification Free

Keep bots and drive-by accounts out. New members pass a human check before they can see your channels.

  • Auto-creates and positions Unverified and Verified roles below the bot
  • Locks channels for unverified members and posts the verify panel
  • Human check powered by Cloudflare Turnstile

/setupverification

Auto-moderation & manual tools Free

Configurable filters plus the everyday moderation commands.

  • Banned words, invite and mention filters with a guided wizard
  • Kick, ban, unban, timeout, untimeout, warn and warning history
  • Mod-log for roles, channels, members, moderation, voice and invites

/automod /kick /ban /timeout /warn /modlog

Anti-raid FreeAnti-raid Pro

Detect join spikes, lock down and alert your staff. Anti-raid Pro adds smarter filtering and cleanup.

  • Free: spike detection, lockdown and staff alerts
  • Pro: join-profile filter (account age, default avatar, suspicious name) that can flag, quarantine or kick
  • Pro: a raid report posted when each raid ends
  • Pro: quarantine for review with Ban all, Release all or review one by one
  • Saved Pro settings pause, rather than vanish, if Premium lapses

/antiraid

Honeypot, Scam Shield & quarantine FreePremium

Extra layers that catch spam bots and hacked accounts.

  • Honeypot: a trap channel that auto-actions spam bots and hacked accounts (free)
  • Honeypot premium extras: choice of action, history window and log channel
  • Scam Shield, join gate and quarantine protections

/honeypot

Leveling, XP & clans FreePaid unlock

Reward activity with XP, illustrated level-up cards and role rewards.

  • Rank and leaderboard, level-role rewards and voice-channel XP
  • Leaderboard buttons: one XP options button holds Boost XP, level-up pings and Buy Boost, and a Dashboard button opens your stats on the web
  • Illustrated level-up cards across many tiers
  • Clans and clan chiefs, and a My clan page on the web dashboard
  • Optional paid XP boosts (personal or whole-server), see Pricing

/rank /leaderboard /levelrole /voicexp

Economy, cards & games FreePremium

Give your community something to do between conversations.

  • Server economy, shop and configuration
  • Trading cards: pull, trade and browse the marketplace
  • Heist Wars operations console, inventory and loadout
  • Inter-server roast battles judged by crowd vote; Premium unlocks Hardcore Roast
  • Giveaways; Premium adds bonus entries, auto-reroll, scheduling and custom colors

/economy /shop /card /heist /roastarena /giveaway

AI chat & images FreePremium

Ask questions, get recommendations and generate images from a prompt.

  • AI chat by command, reply, @mention or DM, with a daily usage meter (6 per day free)
  • Ask for your level or rank and it quotes your real numbers, global rank included, with an Open dashboard button
  • Three characters: Gen Z, Gentle or Sensei, switched by asking in chat
  • Voice replies: the AI may answer with a voice note, or you can ask for one; text is always sent too
  • Reads public http(s) links you share and uses them in its answer
  • Image generation from a text prompt and reverse image search
  • AI Store of paid AI personas
  • Premium raises the daily AI chat limit to 30 per day

/aichat /aiimage /aistatus /imagesearch /aistore

Music, downloads & utilities FreePremium

Voice-channel music and handy lookups.

  • Voice-channel music queue controlled by buttons on a Now Playing panel; tracks are queued from the Download Hub panel
  • Free tier includes 10 listens per day; Music Pro unlocks unlimited plays, uploads and downloads
  • Download audio or video from a supported link, headlines, currency conversion, stock and crypto prices, crypto price alerts

/download /news /convert /stock /crypto /alert

Anime discovery Free

Find something to watch.

  • Browse trending anime by category and search by name
  • Save your own anime categories and submit titles for the catalog

/discover /search /animecategory /submit

Community tools FreePremium

The small things that make a server feel run well.

  • Tickets; Premium adds categories, custom buttons, transcripts and auto-close
  • Application forms: build a form with a guided wizard, members apply in a modal, staff accept or decline in a review channel (free: 1 form, 3 questions, 2 colors; Premium: 10 forms, 5 questions, 10 colors, auto-role on accept)
  • Suggestions, starboard, reaction roles, role setup and link buttons
  • Invite tracker, scheduled messages, auto-responders and announcements
  • Server analytics with growth suggestions, and channel-name styling with fancy fonts
  • Custom roles: members style their own role (name, font, color, icon)

/ticket /application /suggest /starboard /invites /serveranalytics /style /customrole

Get discovered and bring people in.

  • Bump network: bump your server to other opted-in servers
  • Bot directory and Bots Archive
  • Referrals and Top.gg voting (votes give a short XP boost)

/bump /bumpsetup /botstore /archive /referrals
