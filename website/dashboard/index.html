Your server and your stats, in a browser

Your member area Free

Sign in with Discord to see your own numbers across every server the bot is in.

  • My servers: level, XP, rank and coins in each server
  • My rank: your global rank, level and progress, plus your best and lowest server rank
  • Global leaderboard: 10 per page. Your name shows only if you want it to; everyone else appears as Hidden player
  • My clan: your clan in each server, how many members it has, whether you hold a chief seat and what a seat needs
  • Preferences: language, currency, AI character and level-up pings
  • Purchases and Privacy & data, including deleting your dashboard data

Level-up card studio Card plan

Design the card shown when you level up. Anyone can open the editor and preview; saving needs the card plan ($2 per month).

  • Pick a design, upload your own background or logo (checked before use)
  • Live preview on a placeholder avatar
  • Card plan members also get a free website AI chat, 10 messages a week

Server dashboard Free

For people who manage a server. Change the same settings as /serversetup from a browser.

  • Every module's settings, with reset to defaults, export and import
  • Moderation cases and warns, and removing a single warn
  • Analytics: joins and leaves, active members, top XP members and top inviters
  • Giveaways, scheduled messages, welcome card preview and a change history
  • Premium status and checkout for your server

/serversetup

Friends & messages Free

Message friends you met in your servers, from the dashboard.

  • Friend requests, block and report
  • Text only, with Scam Shield and rate limits
  • Turn requests and DM notifications off any time

Developer mode Paid

A workspace for builders on the same sign-in. $5 per month or $30 per year.

  • AI chat on the website, 50 chats a week, with your choice of model
  • Bring your own AI key (Claude, Groq or OpenAI). Keys are encrypted and never shown again
  • Connect GitHub read-only: browse repos, files and diffs, and attach them to a chat
  • Scheduled jobs: reminders, notes and AI prompts on an hourly, daily or weekly schedule
  • Export notes, text or JSON to your Discord DMs

Dashboard links inside Discord Free

The bot now points you to the right dashboard page.

  • Ask the AI for your level or rank and it quotes your real numbers, your global rank included, with an Open dashboard button
  • /leaderboard has a Dashboard button and a single XP options button that holds Boost XP, level-up pings and Buy Boost
  • Dashboard buttons are not shown on clone bots, whose numbers are separate

/aichat /leaderboard

Privacy. The global leaderboard is opt-out: switch it off on the Leaderboard page and you show as Hidden player. No user ID is ever shown to other people. Clan assignment is automatic and can only be viewed here, not changed. Read the Privacy Policy or see the FAQ.
