Billing details

How paying works

Currency & taxes

Prices are in USD. Checkout may show a local-currency equivalent. Any taxes or fees that apply are shown by the payment provider at checkout.

Billing frequency

Monthly: charged every month on Gumroad, or 30 days per payment on Paystack. Yearly: one payment for 365 days. Lifetime and every other unlock: a single payment.

Auto-renewal & cancelling

Gumroad monthly renews automatically and can be cancelled any time; Premium stays active until the end of the period you already paid for. Paystack payments never renew, so there is nothing to cancel.

Delivery

Everything is digital and applied to your Discord server or account after payment is confirmed; there is nothing to ship. If a payment is still pending approval, you can request a full refund before it is approved.
